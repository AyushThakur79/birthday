Happy Birthday ❤️

Toh iss baar I hope main tumhe tumhare birthday pe achhi treat dungi aur purane birthdays ki tarah ye birthday tumhara spoil nahi karungi. 😂

Sabse pehle, I am very lucky ki hum itne time se saath hain, and mujhe bahut achha lagega jab hum future mein bhi aise hi saath honge, baatein karenge aur apne life experiences ek-dusre ke saath share karte rahenge.

I hope abhi jo tum struggle kar rahi ho, woh sab jaldi khatam ho jaaye and tum ek achhi life enjoy karo. Apna best life partner choose karo, jo tumhe waise treat kare jaise tum chahti ho aur jaise tum deserve karti ho. ❤️

And maybe main tumhe kam time deti hoon, utni priority nahi deti, but it doesn't mean ki main tumse kam pyaar karti hoon. Kaafi karti hoon, bas utna show nahi karti, because kabhi-kabhi for granted le leti hoon maybe. But yeah, I'll try, and I am trying ki tumhe hamesha achhe se treat karun.

Aur jaise-jaise life busy hoti ja rahi hai, I hope humein ladne ka time hi nahi milega. Ab tak jitni bhi ladai hui aur humne usko sort kiya, aur tumne bhi apne end se cheezein sort ki, uske liye thank you. Mujhe kaafi achha lagta hai jis tarah se tum meri care karti ho, mere liye stand leti ho aur mere kaam karne pe mujhe pay karti ho. 😂 Abhi tak sirf ₹500 ki payment aayi hai, I hope jaldi baaki payment bhi aa jaayegi.

Baaki birthday party bhi toh deni padegi ab aapko. Toh party mein meri do wishes hain — ek toh main tumhe khila dungi, aur tumse main chahti hoon ki tum mujhe Popeyes ka chicken khila do. Maza aa jaayega. 😂

And obviously, main chahti hoon ki aaj ka din tum mere saath spend karo. But agar tumhara banda aa jaata hai toh I don't have any problem, tum ja sakti ho uske saath. Bas phir bhi mujhe party de dena.

Baaki yes, I love you and thank you for staying by my side.

Itna hi likhna chahti thi main tumhare liye.

Happy Birthday, love you! ❤️
